WHY CRASH2CLAIM

There’s more to an accident than the crash.

01 — THE SCALE

Millions of motor vehicle crashes are reported on U.S. roads every year.

02 — THE AFTERMATH

Afterward, people are often left figuring out what comes next — recovery, medical care, insurance, finances, sometimes legal questions — and where to find honest information they can trust.

03 — THE STORY

Crash2Claim isn’t a law firm, an insurance company, or a source of medical or legal advice. It’s a place for people to share what actually happened, in their own words, so others going through something similar have real experiences to learn from.

Why Crash2Claim Exists

Car accidents happen to real people, but most of what you find online about them is written by companies trying to sell you something. Crash2Claim is different — it's a growing library of first-person accounts from people who've actually been through it.

We're not a law firm, and we don't promise any outcome. We just believe there's value in hearing what really happened, straight from the people who lived it — the confusion, the paperwork, the waiting, and everything in between.
