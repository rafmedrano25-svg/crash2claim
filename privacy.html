Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how 83 Advio LLC ("83 Advio," "Crash2Claim," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit crash2claim.com and use the accident-evaluation questionnaire available on this website (collectively, the "Site"). Crash2Claim is a brand operated by 83 Advio LLC, a Florida limited liability company. By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies only to information collected through the Site. It does not apply to information collected by any third party, including any attorney, law firm, or other business that may receive your information as described below, once that information has been provided to them. Those third parties have their own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review them separately.

3. Information You Provide to Us

When you use the Site, we ask you to provide information about your accident and how to reach you, including:

  • Details about your accident and potential claim, such as the date of the accident, the type of vehicle involved, your intended use of any settlement, whether and how soon you sought treatment after the accident, whether the other party was at fault, whether you had insurance at the time, and whether you already have an attorney.
  • Information about any injuries you experienced.
  • Your contact information, including your first name, last name, phone number, and email address.
  • Your consent to be contacted, including the exact disclosure text shown to you, the date and time you gave consent, and where in the questionnaire that consent was captured.

4. Information We Collect Automatically

As of the effective date of this Privacy Policy, we do not automatically collect your IP address, device or browser information, precise geolocation, date of birth, Social Security number, or payment or financial account information through the Site. If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy to reflect what is actually collected.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

As of the effective date of this Privacy Policy, the Site does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies. If we begin using cookies or similar technologies in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy before doing so.

6. Attribution Information

If you arrive at the Site through an online advertisement or link, we may collect information about that referral, such as campaign or source identifiers included in the link, along with the page you landed on and the page that referred you to the Site. We use this information to understand how consumers find the Site and to evaluate our advertising.

7. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate the Site and the accident-evaluation questionnaire.
  • Evaluate whether your submission meets our preliminary qualification criteria for a potential motor-vehicle-accident claim.
  • Maintain a record of the consent you provided and when.
  • Communicate with you about your submission.
  • Provide your information, where you qualify, to third parties as described in Section 8 below.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights and the rights of others.

8. Commercial Lead Generation — How We Share, Transfer, Sell, or License Your Information

Crash2Claim is a commercial lead-generation business. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice or legal representation.

If your submission meets our preliminary qualification criteria, the information you provide may be sold, transferred, licensed, distributed, shared, or otherwise provided for compensation to qualified third parties. We do not have a single, fixed recipient for every submission. Instead, we work with an open group of potential recipients that may include attorneys, law firms, legal intake companies, legal service providers, lead buyers, marketing partners, and other qualified third-party buyers or service providers. Which recipient, if any, receives your information may depend on factors such as your location, your questionnaire answers, and a recipient's current requirements or availability, and this group of potential recipients may change over time.

Providing your information to these third parties in exchange for compensation may constitute a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under certain state privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act. We describe this practice here directly so you understand how your information may be used.

We may also share information with service providers who help us operate the Site and deliver our services, such as hosting and data-storage providers. These service providers process information on our behalf and are not the same as the third-party recipients described above.

We may also disclose information: to comply with the law or respond to legal process; to protect the safety, property, or rights of Crash2Claim, our users, or others; to prevent or stop illegal or harmful activity; or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of some or all of our assets.

9. Potentially Sensitive Information

The information you provide about your injuries and treatment may be considered sensitive under certain state privacy laws. We use this information to evaluate your submission and, where you qualify and consent, to share it with third parties as described in Section 8. If you have questions about how this information is used or wish to make a request regarding it, contact us using the information in Section 17.

10. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we have collected about you, or to ask us not to sell or share your information going forward. To make a request, contact us at privacy@crash2claim.com. We will respond to verified requests in accordance with applicable law. Because your information may already have been provided to a third-party recipient before we receive your request, we may need to separately notify that recipient of your request, and we cannot guarantee how a third party that already received your information will respond. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy rights available to you.

13. Third-Party Websites

14. Children's Privacy

15. SMS and Telephone Consent Data
